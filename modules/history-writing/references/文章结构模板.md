# Article Structure Templates

First choose the skeleton that can answer this piece's core question. The structures below come from manual annotation of each piece, and do not require every piece to split its paragraphs in the same proportions. Each piece's complete skeleton is in the `structure` field under `corpus/meta/`.

T1 to T6 describe how the whole piece advances; the K1 to K9 in [Opening Hooks](开头钩子.md) describe the mechanisms by which the opening creates expectation, and the same structure can take a different opening. First check whether the body can deliver on this version's promise, then choose the entry point; do not let type numbers replace judgment.

## T1 Fate Retrospective

Evidence: H003, H007, H009, H015, H030, H031, H035; rechecked H022, H044, H038.

Abnormal ending or situation, back to the starting point relevant to the question, two or three key choices, the situation changes accordingly, back to the opening.

The opening may juxtapose treatment before and after, such as a lavish burial against a family's fall from power. The first turning point is not "he was born", but the concrete event that changed the conditions of his fate. The middle writes who did what, making the next step easier or harder. The close explains the change, without treating the ending as "retribution". H007 runs on a prop throughout, and H015 advances through relayed help, showing that this template is not always about a powerful minister dying miserably.

Usable opening: the same person was entrusted with important matters in one phase, and later lost the power to move subordinates on his own. What happened in between? An actual draft must fill in verified concrete posts and events, and cannot copy the empty frame directly.

## T2 Dilemma Breakdown

Evidence: H010, H018, H023, H027, H032, H040, H041, H052 to H055; rechecked H005, H014, H019, H050.

Contradiction, each side's situation, resources, and options, one decision that changes the relationship, the outcome explained by actions or institutions.

Break an abstract judgment into checkable steps. H027's "three ledgers" offers a change of perspective; H041 explains a proxy relationship through dependence; H018 shows a principle through concrete advice. They can share the dilemma structure, but cannot share one answer of "you were too clever, so you had to die".

The counterintuitive openings of H023 and H040 are strong, but the evidence is not enough to support a conspiracy verdict. A new draft can explain "why this reading appeared, and how far the sources support it", but cannot use a suspenseful question to imply a murderer has already been found.

## T3 Thematic Roundup

Evidence: H001, H006, H021, H037, H045; rechecked H008, H036, H056.

Set the comparison standard, give each person one unique action and one consequence, compare the similarities and differences, answer the opening standard.

Two people compared also belong to this type. Choose the standard first, then the people; "clever", "tragic", and "loyal" need to land on dimensions that can be compared. If rankings are used, state at the opening that the basis is a personal judgment, and do not call it an objective historical ranking. Each entry should provide new information; ten people cannot all end with "too much merit, the emperor feared him".

A follow-up piece only continues when it actually exists and the user wants a series; a single piece must deliver its own promise. H001/H008 are one topic cluster, not two pieces of independent evidence.

## T4 Contribution Reassessment

Evidence: H012, H020, H029, H043; rechecked H028.

Familiar label, one contribution on a different scale, the mechanism by which it worked, the cost and limits at the time, a judgment with boundaries.

Enter from "who left what behind" into "why it was able to work". H029 spans Qin, Ming, and the modern era, a reminder that the Great Wall of different ages cannot be merged into one material and one system. H043's cost in its own time against its later legacy suits a two-sided judgment, but "if he had lived a few more years, it would certainly have been so" cannot prove a contribution.

## T5 Object Knowledge (Provisional)

Only H025 and H026, two pieces, not yet enough to assert a stable style.

An object in front of the viewer or an everyday question, natural or engineering constraints, how the design responds, observable results and limits.

Each short draft explains only one system. Forbidden City rainwater drainage and sewage removal are handled separately; do not deny an underground system at the opening and then explain drainage with a hidden channel later. Numbers, construction periods, and "first" titles all need verification. Without visual material, do not claim the camera has already filmed a detail somewhere.

## T6 Folk Story (Provisional)

H024 is a prop puzzle, H057 is myth and folklore, two pieces in total with clear internal differences.

A prop or custom raises a question, the figure tries something, is blocked, changes method or pays a price, and the piece returns to the question.

When telling a folk story, first state the nature of the version; check folklore knowledge against its sources separately. Newly composed dialogue and scenes are allowed when the user asks for them, but must not be attributed as lines from a historical biography, and a folk version must not be generalized into the single nationwide custom.

## Adapting to Word Count (Editing Strategy of the New Skill)

| Target Body | Suggested Amount of Information |
| --- | --- |
| 300 characters or fewer | One question, one action and its consequence, leave out the full life story |
| 301–600 characters | One event, two steps of cause and effect; no ten-person list |
| 601–1200 characters | One main question, two or three pieces of evidence, with room for one disputed boundary |
| 1201–2000 characters | Three or four key moments, or three people with clear distinctions |
| Over 2000 characters | Expand background, counterexamples, and source differences; still centered on one main question |

This is not a statistic of the original authors' proportions. First choose enough real content, then allocate the word count; expansion adds information, shortening removes side threads, and neither relies on dense rhetorical questions to fill space.
