## Delivery Conventions for New Drafts (Editorial Design, Not Distilled Observation)

Voiceover body text is split into short paragraphs by meaning, for ease of reading aloud, without forcing subheadings or a line break per sentence. Titles, source notes, and the body are kept separate. When the user needs storyboards, visual suggestions can be drawn from verified actions, geography, and objects, stated clearly as new design; the writing skill itself does not generate video, and does not claim to have replicated these accounts' visual style.

When screenshots or videos are added later, analyze the role of the visuals by type, such as evidential, narrative, or illustrative, and record the sample IDs. Until supplementary evidence is added, keep this boundary.
