# Topics and Series

## Classification Scheme

Keep one `history-writing`, and do not split a skill per dynasty. Writing can transfer across eras, while facts are searched again by era and figure.

| Dimension | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| era | Main era, one per piece, for total counts | Qin-Han, Ming-Qing, cross-dynasty |
| era_tags | Periods involved, multiple allowed | Western Han, end of Eastern Han, Shu Han, Southern Dynasties, Liu Song, Southern Song |
| people | Figures or core objects | Liu Xun, Huo Guang, the Forbidden City |
| conflict | The conflict of this piece | Talent versus authorization, cost of engineering versus defense needs |
| template | Main structure T1–T6 | Fate retrospective, dilemma breakdown |
| cluster | Same story or highly similar theme | Emperor Xuan of Han and the Huo clan, the death of Han Xin, the fall of a dynasty |
| source_class / risk | Nature of the material and issues pending verification | History mixed with romance, specific dialogue pending verification |

Main era distribution: Pre-Qin 3, Qin-Han 13, Three Kingdoms to Two Jin and Northern and Southern Dynasties 13, Sui-Tang and Wu Zhou 5, Ming-Qing 12, cross-dynasty 9, contemporary figures 1, myth and folklore 1. Multiple tags may overlap, and do not add up to the main era. H029's main era is Qin-Han but spans Qin, Ming, and the modern era. Liu Song is not the same as the Zhao Song, and the tool's "Song dynasty" only expands to Song, Northern Song, and Southern Song, and does not match Liu Song.

## Generating Ideas

First filter by the era, figures, and quantity the user states clearly; with no preference, spread across multiple eras, templates, and story clusters. Read the `risk` in the results, and do not promote the most sensational but weakest-sourced sentence directly into a topic conclusion. Search ranking is by relevance and diversity; likes serve only as sample background, not as a recommendation forecast.

Each card contains: the title; the figures and era; one core question; the factual angle that can be told and the sources to check; the structure; the suggested length; what changed compared with the old draft; the basis ID; and the status. The opening can give a direction sentence, but cannot present an unverified new fact as a confirmed hook.

Sources listed as pending checks during the idea stage are only search leads, not verified material. If a title itself contains an unconfirmed cause of death, dialogue, uniqueness claim, or conspiracy, first change it to a direction that does not assume a conclusion; when writing begins, build a fact-check table per [Historical Facts and Sources](史实与素材.md), and settle the title and hook only after the material holds up.

Use only these statuses:

- **Already in the library**: a corresponding original draft does exist, marked with its ID and likes at collection; this does not mean the content is accurate.
- **New angle on the same topic**: the same figure or event with a different question and evidence path, marked with the related ID; the new angle has no performance verification.
- **Cross-topic transfer**: extended from a structure or cognitive framework to other figures, marked with the structural basis; do not invent that figure's performance in the library.

A Song request can use H037's Bao Zheng, H056's Yue Fei, and H032's Wang Anshi as leads, but cannot claim there is already a large number of standalone Song scripts. New Song topics can transfer T1 to T4, and add facts from History of Song and related research; this is not a way of telling the user to only make topics already in the library.

## Same Topic, Different Writing, and Deduplication

When the user has chosen a topic and explicitly asks "give me several openings to choose from", enter the multi-version flow in [Opening Hooks](开头钩子.md); change the entry point within the same fact pool, defaulting to 3 versions, with a user-specified number taking priority. When only an idea card is wanted, do not expand the full draft; when only alternative openings are wanted, do not continue the body early. The opening mechanisms K1 to K9 are writing labels parallel to dynasty, figure, and whole-piece template, and do not need to be split into nine separate skills.

The same figure may appear in multiple pieces, but at least the core question and the supporting events must change; a title swap alone is not enough. First check `cluster`, then look at the record of topics already used that the user provides. H003/H004/H009/H015 can be divided into deposition, rescue, and power adjustment; H031/H042 cannot merely swap "being used" for "not being trusted". H049/H050 have closely similar frameworks for dynastic collapse, a reminder to avoid applying the same causal chain across different dynasties.

The tool's 5-character fragment overlap only flags repeated wording, and cannot prove independent thought or plagiarism; the overlapping content must be looked at. A popular original draft must not be rewritten wholesale as one's own experience.

## Series Planning

First settle the shared question and how many pieces it can support, then arrange the order. Each piece needs its own information increment and answer. Three ways to organize are "earlier and later stages of a figure", "the same mechanism with different outcomes", and "the same object with different systems". When several consecutive pieces intentionally belong to the same series, the same story cluster is allowed, but each piece must state clearly which event differs from the previous one.

Example: figures around Emperor Xuan of Han can be organized by the prison rescue, the marriage among commoners, the conditions of succession, and the change in the Huo clan's power; this only lists a material plan, and writing follows verification, without carrying an invented scene from an original draft through as a continuous story.

The record of used topics is kept in the user's project directory, with suggested fields of date, title, figures, core question, basis ID, status, and link. Generation only records `draft`; `published` is recorded only after the user confirms actual publication. The skill does not publish on its own or write to external platforms.
