# Story Structure Template: Yuling2711 "Character Stories"

> T-shaped fate flashback structure: throw out the ending / extreme scene first, then develop forward along the timeline.

---

## 1. Standard Single-Episode Structure (T-Shaped Fate Flashback)

```
【钩子层】30-80字
  ↓
  极端场景 / 数字连排 / 结局先行
  → 「您说……怎么会……」设问（或直接）
  → 姓名揭晓（先描述，后点名）

【背景层】100-200字
  ↓
  出生时间+地点+家庭起点
  必含：早年不幸（丧亲/贫困/阶层低）
  + 命运第一次转机（被发现/被选中）

【崛起层】200-300字
  ↓
  才华展现 + 精确数字支撑的成就
  + 巅峰时刻（最光辉的那一刻）
  末尾埋转折悬念：「可谁也没想到……」

【磨难层】200-400字
  ↓
  一场或多场具体磨难（场景还原，非概述）
  + 情感细节（对话/遗言/细节道具）
  + 「您说……」推进共情
  每段磨难之间用「可」「偏偏」「竟」衔接

【结局/升华层】100-200字
  ↓
  结局交代（精确时间+平静语气）
  + 金句升华（真正的X不是Y，而是Z）
  + 传播号召语

【下集悬念】可选，30-50字
  ↓
  预告下集核心矛盾/反转
  + 「下集见」
```

---

## 2. Writing Rules for Each Layer

### Hook Layer (see "Opening Hook DNA" in detail)
- Length: 30-80 characters (Episode 1 slightly shorter; sequels can run slightly longer to include a recap of the previous episode)
- Core action: create a "how is this possible" cognitive gap
- The name is revealed at the latest in the second sentence; it cannot be stated in the very first breath

### Background Layer
- Required elements: exact birth year, birthplace, and which child she was in the family (if applicable)
- Required misfortune: extreme poverty / losing a parent young / very low social standing
- Narrative tone: calm statement, not sentimental; let the facts speak for themselves
- Pace: short sentences, 2-3 details are enough, no spreading out

### Rise Layer
- Core task: make the audience understand how remarkable this person was, so they can feel the later fall
- Must have exact numbers (age, year, award names, film titles)
- The contrast pattern "that little girl from..., who stood on..." appears frequently
- The end cannot lack a turning hook like "but no one expected"; without it, the structure breaks

### Hardship Layer
- No summary; scenes must be reconstructed:
- ❌ "She suffered serious domestic violence"
- ✅ "Her suicide note reads: if you had not hit me that night, and hit me again tonight, I probably would not have done this"
- Emotional detail props (the earring clutched in her hand, the ashes split into two portions, pretending she could also hear the hallucinations)
- When several hardships stack, escalate with "that was not enough / and that was not even the worst of it"
- "Tell me..." appears when the hardship builds to its peak, detonating the emotion

### Ending Layer
- Death / passing is told in a calm tone, with an exact time
- "On March 8, 1935, 25-year-old Ruan Lingyu left forever"
- "On July 21, 2000, 58-year-old Yang Likun passed away peacefully at home in Shanghai"
- The golden line comes after the ending, not before it
- The share call to action must contain "deserves to be known by more people" or "let more people know"

## 3. Three-Episode Series Pacing Template

### Episode 1: Identity Building ("Who this person is, and how remarkable she was")
- Focus: talent on display plus peak achievements
- Ending: hardship cliffhanger ("But just then, a storm...")
- Structure ratio: background 20% / rise 50% / hardship preview 30%
- Share call to action: stress that "this kind of genius deserves to be known by more people"

### Episode 2: Hardship Deep Dive ("What she went through")
- Focus: concrete details of the darkest period, highest emotional density
- Opening: continues Episode 1, with "last time we left off..." or a direct cut into the darkest scene
- Ending: cliffhanger ("But the story is not over yet...") or a tearful golden line
- Structure ratio: hardship core 70% / struggle and turning point 20% / next episode preview 10%
- Share call to action: stress "let more women know..."

### Episode 3: Ending / Reversal ("How it ended in the end")
- Focus: often a surprising angle (the husband's endurance / descendants guarding her memory / rehabilitation by the times / how later generations remember her)
- Opening: sequel bridge, or the most moving detail thrown out directly
- Ending: strongest golden line plus the most emotionally charged share call to action
- Structure ratio: ending reversal 50% / emotional elevation 30% / golden line plus call to action 20%

## 4. Three Variants of the Share Call to Action (Interchangeable)

```
A. 知识/天才型
「[如果你觉得这个姑娘了不起/这种坚守值得敬佩]，
就帮我传出去，不是为了给谁涨粉，
是[人物名+核心价值标签]值得被更多人知道。」

B. 情感共鸣型
「如果你身边也有[相似境遇的人]，
把这个故事转给他看看。
[人物名]的故事值得被更多人听见。」

C. 历史正名型（用于末集，激活使命感）
「[人物名]不该被遗忘，
你帮我传一传，让更多人知道这个名字。」
```

---

## 5. Structural Taboos

- ❌ Opening by stating the person's name directly ("Today I will tell you about Tian Hua...")
- ❌ Flat structure: a life timeline told straight through, with no ups and downs
- ❌ Summary in the hardship layer instead of scenes ("She went through many difficulties")
- ❌ Golden line placed in the middle of the hardship layer; it belongs as the finale after the ending
- ❌ No "Tell me" driving line (without it, the emotional anchor is missing)
- ❌ Share call to action omitted or reduced to "like and follow"
